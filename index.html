About XSS TXD

XSS TXD is a free online editor for GTA San Andreas and SA-MP files. It runs fully in the browser of your phone (iOS or Android) or computer — nothing to install, and the files you upload never leave your device. Here you can change vehicle and ped textures and models, adjust the sky and weather look, edit weapon, vehicle and ped stats, and change your character's animations.

What it can do

  • TXD / DFF Editor — edit textures: add text and images, brush and eraser, Layers panel, and DXT compression to shrink the file.
  • 3D Preview — see the model in 3D while you edit, including Ped Pose for skin / ped models.
  • UV Editor — fix how the texture is mapped onto the model.
  • 3D Objects (BETA) — add simple 3D parts like a spoiler, bullbar, roof rack and light bar, and bake them into the downloaded DFF.
  • Timecyc Editor (BETA) — edit sky, sun, cloud and water colors for every weather and time, with batch edit and presets like Golden Hour and Sunset Vibes.
  • Colorcycle Editor (BETA) — adjust the color grading of the mobile version of the game.
  • Cheats — Weapon Editor (53 guns), Handling Editor (210 vehicles, with Drift / Race / Off-road / Realistic presets), and Ped Editor (43 entries).
  • Animation Editor (BETA) — swap and edit movement in ped.ifp: rotate the bones and try the one-tap Naruto Run preset.
  • Sticker packs — Logos, Flames and Tribal stickers you can place, drag, resize and rotate on a texture.
  • ZIP download / Download All — download all your edited files at once.

How to use it

  1. Tap Enter above to open the editor.
  2. Upload your DFF and TXD files in the editor — or open the Timecyc, Colorcycle, Cheats or Animation Editor from the ••• menu.
  3. Edit the texture, model, or values — you see the result in the preview right away.
  4. Download the edited file and put it in your game folder, the same way you install any mod.

Supported files

  • DFF — 3D model of vehicles, peds and other objects
  • TXD — the texture file that goes with the DFF
  • timecyc.dat — weather, sky and time of day
  • colorcycle.dat — color grading (mobile)
  • weapon.dat — weapon stats
  • handling.cfg — vehicle speed and handling
  • pedstats.dat — ped behavior and stats
  • ped.ifp — character animations / movement

XSS TXD works on iOS, Android and PC, and supports all GTA SA and SA-MP launchers / APKs. Your edits are saved in your browser session, so even if the page reloads, your work comes back.
